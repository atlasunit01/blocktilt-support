BlockTilt support

Help with controls, power-ups, Game Center, and purchases.

BlockTilt is free to download with ads between runs. The optional No Ads purchase removes in-game advertising for a one-time US$2.99; the App Store shows the price for your region before purchase.

How to play

  1. Choose an angle. The direction line swings automatically. Tap the course once to lock it.
  2. Choose your power. Opacity builds along the direction line. A more opaque line means greater power. Tap again to launch.
  3. Let the block bounce. Gravity and friction take over. Your score is the furthest line reached; touching a marked fifth line grants a random power-up.
  4. Try another run. When the block settles, leaves the slope, or falls through vertical lines, the run ends. Tap the course to reset.

The three bottom icons open Play, Leaderboard, and Settings. They appear before a throw and after a run, and disappear after you lock the angle. Moving the app to the background pauses the run and its effect timers.

Power-ups

Power-up names and countdown circles appear in the center of the screen. Different types can overlap; a new shape or gravity effect replaces the previous effect of the same type.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. Your local personal best is saved on your device even when you are disconnected.

To connect, open Settings in BlockTilt and choose Sign In. Apple handles sign-in. After connecting, complete a run to submit a score, then open Leaderboard to choose Global or Friends.

If a connected player's score cannot be submitted, the app keeps that player's best pending score on the device and retries for the same Game Center account when a connection is available. Runs played while disconnected are not later assigned to another account.

Disconnect from BlockTilt stops Game Center use in the app. It does not sign your Apple account out or delete scores already sent to Game Center. To sign out of the account, open iPhone Settings → Game Center → Sign Out.

If a board is empty, check your connection and the message shown in the app. Friends results depend on your Game Center friends and their available scores. See how Game Center data is handled.

Ads between runs

An ad may appear after a completed run once you have accumulated at least 120 seconds of active play. Choosing power and the block's flight count toward that time. Waiting before a throw, browsing menus, background time, ads, and purchase screens do not count.

Ads do not interrupt a run. If an ad is unavailable, you can keep playing; the app can try again after a later completed run. After an ad appears, another 120 seconds of active play must accrue before the next opportunity.

Google AdMob supplies the ads. Privacy messages may appear when required. Use Ad Privacy Choices in Settings when available to review those choices. Read how advertising data is handled.

No Ads and restoring purchases

Open Settings and choose No Ads to make a one-time purchase through Apple. There is no subscription. The current local price is shown before you confirm payment. Ads stop when Apple verifies the purchase.

If you already own No Ads, sign in to the App Store account used for the purchase and choose Restore Purchases in BlockTilt Settings. Apple may ask you to authenticate. Reinstalling the app does not require buying it again when the same account has a valid purchase.

A canceled or pending purchase does not unlock No Ads. If the store is unavailable or verification fails, check your connection and try Restore Purchases. A refunded or revoked purchase may no longer remove ads. Do not send passwords or payment-card details to support.

Appearance, sound, and haptics

The game follows your iPhone's light or dark appearance automatically: a black block on white, or a white block on black. Sound and haptics can be turned on or off in BlockTilt Settings.
